Marble Race Module catalog: what the removal cost, and what to build next

The five removed Modules were exactly one per Role. The catalog went from two Modules in every Role to one in every Role, which collapses selectRoleModules() from 32 possible Role selections to 1 — every race now draws the same four shapes, and only their order varies. Four new Modules, one per obstacle Role, restore combinatorial variety.

Why the five died — and where the candidates sit

Positions are design judgements, not measurements. No Validator Dwell Time numbers were pulled for this page — the vertical axis is an assessment of whether geometry guarantees an exit, not a recorded worst case. Run the Validator before trusting any candidate's vertical position.

Build order

First
Crossover weave
shuffle
Lanes bridge over and under each other, inverting relative order. Static geometry, guaranteed exit, swap is legible face-on. Highest value per unit of risk.
Second
Turnstile
queue
A 3- or 4-armed cross in a throat, admitting one marble per arm. Dwell bounded at arms × period. Reuses KinematicRotationMotion as-is.
Third
Bumper cluster
scatter
A few large high-restitution bumpers: few contacts, huge deflections. The opposite texture to pin-field's fine diffusion. Convex, no basin.
Fourth
Cascading ledges
sort
Offset shelves where fast marbles fly the gap and slow ones ride down. A speed sort whose criterion the viewer can actually see.

These four take Role selections from 1 to 16 (2 × 2 × 2 × 2 across the obstacle Roles) without touching arc.ts, assembleCourse.ts, or the registry contract.

Held back, with reasons

Zipper merge queue
Two lanes combing into one sits close to the removed funnel-choke. Build it only if the comb geometry provably guarantees clearance; if you cannot prove it, you are re-shipping R4.
Loaded rocker sort
A see-saw tipping under marble weight is contact-driven, so step(spec, t) could no longer be pure in t. It needs a dynamic body, which splits the Validator's world from the renderer's. A deliberate contract change, not a Module.
Pocketed drum shuffle
Great drama, but if pockets are fewer than the field size the overflow queues outside the drum, making it part queue. It earns the shuffle tag only after a Validator sweep says so.
Splitter tree scatter
Three levels of wedges fanning into eight lanes reads as discrete choices rather than noise. The risk is a marble balancing on a wedge apex — round or bias it before committing.

One contract change worth making

ColliderSpec.motion admits only KinematicRotationMotion. Adding a translation or oscillation kind stays pure in t, costs little, and unlocks sweeping gate bars, shuttle platforms, and launcher pistons — a class of legible moving obstacles the Spec currently cannot express.
