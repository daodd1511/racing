Under consideration

The shortlist, and the whole of it: two scatter Modules picked on 2026-08-25, plus the Crossover weave carried over from an earlier round. Everything else was dropped — not because the ideas were wrong, but because a shortlist you can hold in your head is worth more than a catalogue you scroll past.

Break table and Anvil share a front end. Both open with a throat that squeezes the pack into single file — one to fire it into an open pan, one to aim it at a plate. That is one piece of geometry worth building once and parameterising, not twice. It also means the two Modules will read as related on camera even though what happens after the throat is completely different, and that only matters if both ever appear in the same race — which, with selectRoleModules(seed) choosing one Module per Role for the whole Course, they currently cannot. Building both and letting one race show Break table early and Anvil late is a change to the selection in src/course/arc.ts, not to either Module.

Crossover weave

shuffle
Plan view — looking at the Board face-on from above; travel left→right, channel width top↔bottom

What you'd see: two channels braid past each other in the middle of the Module. A marble that entered on the near lane leaves on the far lane, and vice versa. Nothing moves — the swap is pure geometry, so it never jams and never stalls.

Moving parts
None. step() returns [].
Dwell
Bounded by path length; no basin, no meeting point.
Params
Lane count (2–3), crossing length, bridge rise (≈40 mm — clears a 32 mm marble underneath).
Effect on the field — order inverts, gaps unchanged

Break table

scatter
Plan view — looking at the Board face-on from above; travel left→right, channel width top↔bottom

What you'd see: a pool break. The pack is squeezed into a single-file throat, fired into an open pan, and the marbles scatter off each other — the first one in gets hit by the second, and the spread cascades from there. Nothing in the catalogue does this: every other Module treats each marble independently, so a race with three marbles looks like a race with fifteen. Here the field size is the mechanism, and a 15-marble race genuinely looks different from a 5-marble one.

Moving parts
None. step() returns [].
Dwell
Bounded — the pan runs downhill at COURSE_GRADE and the exit is the full channel width, so there is nothing to queue behind.
Params
Throat width (1–2 marble ⌀), pan length, pan width, exit flare.
Watch: the throat is the only risky part — it is a choke by definition. Single-file is the point, so accept the queue but bound it: the pan must drain faster than the throat fills, or the Module becomes the Turnstile. Check the last marble's Dwell in a 15-marble Validator run, not the first's.

Anvil

scatter
Plan view — looking at the Board face-on from above; travel left→right, channel width top↔bottom

What you'd see: the pack funnelled into a line, aimed at a single angled plate, and thrown sideways across the whole channel in one hit. Where the Pin field diffuses the field over twenty small contacts, this spends the entire scatter budget in one frame — the strongest single moment on this page, and the easiest to cut a replay around.

Moving parts
None. One cuboid plate plus two throat walls.
Dwell
Bounded and the shortest on this page — one contact, then a straight run-out.
Params
Plate angle (10–25° off square), plate offset, throat width, run-out length.
Watch: the whole field hits the same plate at nearly the same spot, so the marble behind arrives while the one in front is still there. That is a pile-up, not a scatter, unless the plate's angle carries each marble clear before the next lands — which depends on arrival rate, so the throat length and the plate angle have to be tuned together. Validate with 15 marbles arriving as a block, which is the worst case and also the normal case.

Scale and conventions. Every panel is drawn from the constants in src/race/scale.ts — 32 mm marble, 0.5 m channel, 0.1 m Cell pitch — and Module travel spans are consistent with the 0.6–1.8 m range the live Modules occupy. Plan views look at the Board face-on from above, so the vertical axis is channel width; elevations are the viewer's face-on view, so the vertical axis is gravity.
Status. All three are unbuilt proposals — the Crossover weave from the 2026-08-24 brainstorm, Break table and Anvil drawn 2026-08-25. Dimensions, friction and restitution values, and every dwell claim are design intent, not Validator measurements — run each through the Validator before trusting any of them. Live Module drawings are schematic, not traced from their buildSpec output.
Scope. This page carries scatter Modules only, at the user's direction: scatter is what makes a race worth watching, and it fills 4 of the Arc's 24 Slots against 3 each for shuffle and sort. Everything not on the shortlist was removed on 2026-08-25 — twenty-nine proposals across three rounds, including the Ricochet box, Domed island, and Colander stack drawn the same day. Git history holds every one of them.
What changed on 2026-08-25. The catalogue was cut to four obstacles in fe48df4 (steep zigzag, rumble strip, friction lanes, funnel choke, vortex bowl removed) and the windmill followed in 2be90ca, taking the queue Role with it. So the Arc now has exactly one Module per Role and enumerateRoleSelections() yields one Course. The eleven new candidates are drawn against that Course — 0.5 m channel, COURSE_GRADE 0.12, entry and exit on one continuous centreline — rather than against the Showcase, which is why none of them ask for a free fall, a wider bed, or a second Role.
